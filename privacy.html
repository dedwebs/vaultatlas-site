Vault Atlas Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Overview

Vault Atlas is an offline-first companion app. We do not require an account to use core features.

Data on your device

Saved builds, favorites, recent searches, fishing progress markers, and cached wiki lookups are stored locally on your device.

Network

If you use online lookup, the app requests publicly available wiki/community pages and may cache the result on your device. Purchase processing for the Theorycraft Pack is handled by Apple In-App Purchase.

Analytics / tracking

Vault Atlas does not include third-party advertising SDKs or analytics trackers in the current build.
