Vault Atlas Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Overview

Vault Atlas is an offline-first companion app. You don't need an account. We (the developer, NixonMade) don't run servers that receive your data. We don't sell data, and we don't track you across apps or websites.

Data stored on your device

Saved builds, favorites, recent searches, quest and checklist progress, fishing catch log, wishlist and collection marks, alert preferences, and cached wiki lookups and images are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. We can't see or recover them.

Notifications

If you turn on event alerts, the app asks for iOS notification permission and schedules local notifications (for example, five minutes before a public event). They're created on your device. No push service, device token, or server is involved. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings → Vault Atlas → Notifications.

Online wiki lookup

Search works offline. When a topic isn't in the offline pack, or when a preview image is shown, the app sends the search text or topic title to the community Fallout Wiki (fallout.wiki), and loads images from its image hosts (such as wiki.gg or Wikimedia). Like any website, those services receive your IP address and the request. Their own privacy policies apply. Results and images are cached on your device. We don't receive these requests.

In-app purchases

The Theorycraft Pack is sold through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes payment. We don't receive your payment details or Apple ID, only Apple's confirmation that the purchase is valid on your device.

Crash and diagnostic data

Vault Atlas may use Apple's MetricKit, which gathers crash, hang, and performance diagnostics on your device. The app doesn't upload these reports anywhere; they stay on your device. Apple may share diagnostics with developers only if you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements. These contain no name, account, location, or advertising identifier.

Separately, if you share TestFlight feedback or crash reports, Apple delivers them to us under TestFlight's terms. That includes whatever screenshots, comments, and email you choose to include.

Analytics and tracking

Vault Atlas contains no advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics, and no tracking. It doesn't use the advertising identifier.

Children

Vault Atlas isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
